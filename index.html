The Framework

The S.C.A.M. Framework

Four moves every scam relies on. Once someone can name them, they can stop them — for themselves, and for the people they love.

S

Sudden Pressure

A crisis, a deadline, a reason it can't wait until tomorrow.

C

Communication Tricks

A voice, a name, a story engineered to sound exactly right.

A

Ask to Move Money

Gift cards, wires, apps — always fast, always final.

M

Missing Verification

No callback, no second source, no time to check.
